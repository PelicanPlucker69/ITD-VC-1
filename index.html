How it works

From meeting to movement in three steps

  1. 1

    Meet

    Host or join a call like you always do. LYNK handles the video, audio and captions.

  2. 2

    Move

    When the meeting wraps up, a countdown starts and everyone plays a quick challenge, like finding a household object and snapping a photo.

  3. 3

    Link up

    The fastest time wins. Scores hit the leaderboard and your rewards add up.
